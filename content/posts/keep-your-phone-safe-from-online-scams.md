Scammers have moved to where we spend most of our time: our phones. A few habits make a huge difference.

## 1. Never share an OTP

No bank, delivery company or government office will ask for your OTP. If someone does, it is a scam.

## 2. Don't approve payment requests you don't recognise

On UPI, you never need to enter your PIN to *receive* money. A request asking for your PIN is a request to *take* money.

## 3. Install apps only from official stores

Apps sent as files on chat can take control of your phone. Stick to official app stores.

## 4. Turn on two-factor authentication

Add a second step to your email, social media and banking apps. It stops most account takeovers.

## 5. Report scams quickly

If you lose money, act fast. Report it on the [National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal](https://cybercrime.gov.in/) or call the helpline 1930.

> When in doubt, hang up and call your bank using the number printed on your card.
