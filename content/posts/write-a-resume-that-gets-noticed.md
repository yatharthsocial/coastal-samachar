A strong resume is short, specific and tailored to the job. Here's how to write one.

## Keep it to one page

Unless you have more than ten years of experience, one page is enough.

## Lead with results

Instead of "Responsible for sales", write "Grew monthly sales by 25% in six months". Numbers stand out.

## Match the job description

Many companies use software to filter resumes. Use the same keywords the job posting uses — honestly.

## Use a clean layout

- A simple font and clear headings
- No photos, tables or graphics that confuse software
- Save as PDF unless asked otherwise

## Check it twice

Spelling mistakes are the easiest way to be rejected. Ask a friend to proofread it.
