Being registered is the first step to voting. Here is how to check your details quickly.

## Step 1: Open the official voter portal

Visit the [Election Commission's voter services portal](https://voters.eci.gov.in/). You can search by your EPIC (voter ID) number or by your personal details.

## Step 2: Search for your entry

Enter your name, age, state and district, or simply your EPIC number. If you are registered, your details and polling station will appear.

## Step 3: Note your polling booth

Write down your part number and serial number. These make things faster on election day.

## Step 4: Fix any mistakes

If your name, address or photo is wrong, you can apply for a correction online. Moving house? Apply to shift your registration to the new constituency.

## Common questions

**Can I vote without a voter ID card?** If your name is on the roll, other approved photo ID documents are usually accepted.

**I turned 18 recently. What should I do?** Apply as a new voter as early as possible so your name is added before the next election.
