Two very different events make this a busy weekend in Mangaluru.

## Saturday: DevFest 2026 Mangalore

**GDG Cloud Mangalore** (Google Developer Groups) hosts **DevFest 2026** on **Saturday, 14 November**, from **8 am to 5 pm** at **wrkwrk Triangle, Derebail**. Built around the theme *People, Ideas and Impact*, it promises talks, hands-on learning and networking for developers, students and tech enthusiasts.

- **Tickets:** ₹300 (group discount) or ₹500 (regular)
- **Register:** [konfhub.com](https://konfhub.com/gdg-cloud-mangalore-devfest-2026)

## Sunday: Haleyangadi Seawalk Run, Season 2

The **Haleyangadi Seawalk Run** returns on **Sunday, 15 November**, along the Haleyangadi Sea Walk beside the Arabian Sea. It is organised by **Sri Vidya Vinayaka Yuvaka Mandala (R.), Haleyangadi**, with Surathkal Beach Runners.

| Race | Start | Fee |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10K (Open and Veteran 50+) | 6:00 am | ₹350 |
| 5K (Open) | 6:30 am | ₹250 |
| 2K Saree Run (women, 18+) | 7:30 am | ₹100 |

- **Register:** [sbr.realseclabs.com](https://sbr.realseclabs.com/events/hsr2026)
