**Alva's Nudisiri-Virasat 2027** brings the **17th Alva's Nudisiri** and the **31st Alva's Virasat** together in **Moodbidri** from **6 to 10 January 2027**. Here is your guide, based on the programme announced by Alva's Education Foundation chairman **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** ([NewsKarnataka](https://newskarnataka.com/mangaluru/alvas-nudisiri-virasat-to-be-held-in-moodbidri-from-january-6-to-10/09102026/), [Daijiworld](https://daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1328964)).

## At a glance

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Dates** | 6–10 January 2027 |
| **Place** | Alva's campus, Moodbidri, Dakshina Kannada |
| **Theme** | *Kannada Manassu: Bhavishyada Kanasu* |
| **Editions** | 17th Nudisiri · 31st Virasat |

## Day by day

- **6 January — Virasat opens:** a cultural chariot procession, a Ganga Aarti by a troupe from Haridwar, and two Virasat Awards for eminent artistes in classical and folk traditions.
- **8 January — Alva's Vidyarthisiri:** the student festival.
- **9 January — Alva's Yuvasiri:** the youth festival.
- **Nudisiri literary convention:** inaugurated by senior journalist **Vishweshwar Bhat** and presided over by literary critic **Narahalli Balasubrahmanya**.

## Also at the festival

- **State-level Scouts and Guides Jamboree**, with more than **5,000** participants expected.
- **Five exhibitions** on agriculture, science, food, literature and art.
- **Free fine arts training** for about **800 artists**.
- **Hasiru Samvada**, a coastal river conservation initiative covering 50 villages.

## Why it matters for Alva's students

For students at [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) and the foundation's other schools, Nudisiri-Virasat is a chance to perform, volunteer and learn alongside writers and artists from across Karnataka — through Scouts and Guides, cultural programmes and the student festival days.
