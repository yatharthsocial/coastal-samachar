If your child is moving to high school next year, [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) in Moodbidri is one of the best-known options in Dakshina Kannada. This guide brings together what the school has published about classes, facilities and admissions.

## About the school

Alva's English Medium High School started in **2011–12** on the Alva's Education Foundation campus at **Vivekanandanagara, Puthige, Moodubidire**. The foundation is chaired by **Dr. M. Mohan Alva**. The school reports a **100% SSLC pass record**, with toppers in earlier years scoring up to 618 out of 625.

## What your child gets

- **Small classes:** a maximum of 35 students per class.
- **IIT foundation coaching** alongside the regular syllabus.
- **Extra support:** special coaching and counselling for students who need it.
- **A vacation crash course** for students promoted to Standard IX.
- **Sports:** a 400-metre synthetic track and dedicated coaches.
- **Arts and culture:** classical and folk arts, dance and music.
- **Hostels, a 24×7 medical facility and transport.**

## Sports quota: step by step

1. **Watch for the notification** in newspapers in March–April.
2. **Check eligibility:** Standards 8 to 10; athletics (boys and girls) or basketball (girls).
3. **Apply** with a filled bio-data form and attested copies of certificates.
4. **Attend the selection camp:** about 20 days at the foundation, with boarding and coaching.
5. **If selected,** your child receives free food, accommodation and coaching.

## Documents to keep ready

- Previous year's marks card and transfer certificate
- Birth certificate and Aadhaar
- Passport-size photographs
- Sports certificates (for the sports quota)

*Requirements can change each year, so confirm the final list with the school office.*
