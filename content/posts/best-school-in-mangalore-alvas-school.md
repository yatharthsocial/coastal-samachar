Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a family makes. For many parents searching for the **best school in Mangalore**, the Alva's Education Foundation campus in Moodbidri is high on the list. This video takes you inside.

## Inside the campus

The video opens with sweeping aerial views of the Alva's Education Foundation campus, set among the green hills of Moodbidri in Dakshina Kannada. The campus is home to several schools:

- **Alva's Kindergarten** — a bright, colourful space for the youngest learners.
- **Alva's English Medium School** and **Alva's Kannada Medium School**, giving families a choice of medium of instruction.
- **Alva's Central School**, housed in its own large campus building.

## Learning beyond textbooks

The film shows students in science and computer laboratories, working with an abacus, exploring a space-themed learning room and solving problems at the board. The focus is on curiosity, hands-on learning and confidence.

## Values and discipline

Scenes of morning assembly show students in uniform standing together with folded hands — a reminder that good schooling is about values and discipline as much as marks.

## What parents should look for in a school

Whichever school you choose, these questions help:

1. **Academics:** What are the board results like, and how is each child supported?
2. **Facilities:** Are there labs, a library, sports grounds and safe transport?
3. **Teachers:** How experienced are they, and what is the student-teacher ratio?
4. **Activities:** Are there sports, arts, culture and clubs?
5. **Values:** Does the school build discipline, kindness and confidence?
