**Seventeen students** of **Alva's Pre-University College, Moodbidri**, have qualified for IIT admission through **JEE Advanced 2026**, [Karavali Daily News reported](https://www.karavalidailynews.com/2026/06/02/jee-advanced-dr-mohan-alva-praises-the-unprecedented-achievement-of-moodbidri-alva-students/). Alva's Education Foundation president **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** congratulated the students.

## Top ranks

| Student | Rank |
| --- | --- |
| Swathi Talwar | 1513 |
| Harshavardhan KP | 1698 |
| Melroy Castelino | 1733 |
| Kritik B | 1795 |
| Neelambika | 2223 |

## Starting early

[Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) offers **IIT foundation coaching** from high school, giving students a head start before they move to Alva's PU College on the same campus.
