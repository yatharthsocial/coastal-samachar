For students looking for a quality engineering college near Mangalore, **Alva's Institute of Engineering and Technology (AIET)** in Moodbidri is one of the region's best-known names. Watch the video above for a tour of the college's departments, labs, placements and campus life.

## AIET at a glance

- **Established:** 2008, by Alva's Education Foundation.
- **Location:** Shobhavana Campus, Mijar, Moodbidri — about 33 km from Mangaluru.
- **University:** Affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.
- **Status:** An autonomous institute, approved by AICTE.
- **Accreditation:** Accredited by NAAC with an **A+** grade. Programmes including Computer Science and Engineering and Electronics and Communication Engineering are NBA-accredited.
- **Campus:** A large residential campus of around 144 acres.

## Courses

According to the college, AIET offers B.E. programmes in:

- Computer Science and Engineering
- Computer Science (IoT, Cyber Security and Blockchain Technology)
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Computer Science and Design
- Computer Science and Data Science
- Information Science
- Electronics and Communication
- Mechanical
- Civil
- Agriculture

Check the [official AIET website](https://www.aiet.org.in/) for the latest list of courses and intake.

## Inside the video

The college's video takes viewers through:

- **Labs and centres:** an NVIDIA lab for computer science, a Toyota Centre of Excellence for mechanical engineering, a hydroponic lab for agricultural engineering, and electronics labs.
- **Student start-ups and projects:** students building robots, drones and electronics projects.
- **Placements:** the college says more than 200 companies visit the campus, with highest packages of ₹20 LPA (Amazon) and ₹21 LPA (Juspay) shown in the video.
- **Campus life:** 42 student clubs, NCC, sports, Republic Day celebrations and the *Alva's Virasat* cultural festival.
- **Rankings:** the video highlights a **134th All India rank** in the Times Engineering rankings.

## Why students choose Alva's

### A complete campus

Being part of the Alva's Education Foundation campus means students have access to hostels, sports facilities, libraries and a busy calendar of cultural events, including the foundation's well-known festivals such as *Alva's Virasat* and *Alva's Nudisiri*.

### Autonomy

As an autonomous institute, AIET can update its syllabus and assessments more quickly to keep pace with industry needs.

### Clubs, events and life beyond class

Technical symposiums, student clubs, sports and cultural programmes give students the chance to build teamwork and leadership skills alongside their degree.

## How to get admission

Admission to B.E. programmes in Karnataka is mainly through:

1. **KCET** — counselling by the [Karnataka Examinations Authority](https://cetonline.karnataka.gov.in/kea/). AIET's **CET code is E169**.
2. **COMEDK UGET** — for seats under the COMEDK quota. AIET's **COMEDK code is E006**.
3. **Management quota** — directly through the college, as per government rules.

Keep your marks cards, rank card and category certificates ready, and check the official counselling websites for deadlines.
