The **Matsyagandha Express** between **Mangaluru Central** and **Mumbai Lokmanya Tilak Terminus** will be downgraded from superfast to **mail/express** from **January 6**, [Daijiworld reported](https://daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1334766), citing a South Western Railway release.

## New train numbers

| Old number | New number | Route |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12619 | 16369 | Mumbai LTT → Mangaluru Central |
| 12620 | 16370 | Mangaluru Central → Mumbai LTT |

## Lower fares

With the superfast surcharge removed, passengers will save:

- **General second class:** ₹15
- **Sleeper:** ₹30
- **AC three-tier, AC economy and AC two-tier:** ₹45
- **First AC:** ₹75

## Why the change

The train did not meet the **55 km/h average speed** needed for the superfast category, according to the report. No timing changes were announced.
