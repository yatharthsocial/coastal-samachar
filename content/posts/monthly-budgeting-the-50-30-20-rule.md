Budgeting doesn't need spreadsheets with fifty rows. The 50-30-20 rule is a simple starting point.

## The rule

- **50% for needs** – rent, groceries, bills, loan EMIs.
- **30% for wants** – eating out, shopping, trips.
- **20% for savings** – emergency fund, investments, insurance.

## An example

On a take-home salary of ₹40,000:

| Bucket | Share | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Needs | 50% | ₹20,000 |
| Wants | 30% | ₹12,000 |
| Savings | 20% | ₹8,000 |

## Make it automatic

Set up an automatic transfer to savings on salary day. What you don't see, you won't spend.

## Adjust to your life

If rent is high, needs may take 60%. That's fine — the key is to save *something* every month.
