**Disha** of **Alva's PU College, Moodbidri**, scored a perfect **600 out of 600** to secure the **first rank in the state in Commerce** in the Karnataka II PUC 2026 examinations, [Daijiworld reported](https://www.daijiworld.com/news/newsDisplay?newsID=1311438).

The results were announced on Thursday, 9 April 2026.

## Alva's in the rank list

Alva's students also featured in the Science rank list. [Shiksha's 2026 toppers list](https://www.shiksha.com/boards/articles/karnataka-2nd-puc-toppers-list-2026-arts-science-commerce-check-toppers-name-marks-blogId-226375) names **Rakshita Manjunath Sanganapeth** of Alva's PU College with **598 marks**.

## Dakshina Kannada leads again

Dakshina Kannada and Udupi recorded the highest pass percentages in the state, according to [Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/karnataka-2nd-puc-toppers-list-2026-kseab-udupi-dakshina-kannada-highest-yadgir-lowest-science-commerce-arts-karresults-nic-in/amp).

## From Alva's High School to PU

Many Alva's PU students come from [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) on the same Moodbidri campus, which has a 100% SSLC pass record.
