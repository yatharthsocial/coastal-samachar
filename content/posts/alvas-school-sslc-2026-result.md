**Shadhvi P V** of Alva's School, Moodbidri, scored **622 out of 625** in the SSLC 2026 examinations, [Mangalore Today reported](https://www.mangaloretoday.com/main/SSLC-2026-results-Dakshina-Kannada-tops-Karnataka-with-98-40-Udupi-student-scores-perfect-625.html).

The results were announced on Thursday, 23 April. **Dakshina Kannada** finished first in the state with a pass percentage of **98.40%**.

## A record of 100% results

[Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) has reported a 100% SSLC pass record since its first batch in 2011–12. According to the school, its toppers in earlier years scored up to **618 out of 625 (98.88%)**:

| Year | Topper | Marks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2014–15 | Gokul Reddy K C | 618 (98.88%) |
| 2015–16 | Kavya Aradhya | 618 (98.88%) |

## What helps Alva's students score high

- **Small classes** of up to 35 students.
- **IIT foundation coaching** alongside the syllabus.
- **Extra coaching and counselling** for students who need support.
- **A vacation crash course** for students promoted to Standard IX.

## After SSLC

Many Alva's students continue at **Alva's PU College** on the same campus — where, in II PUC 2026, [Disha topped Karnataka in Commerce with 600/600](/news/alvas-pu-college-disha-tops-karnataka-commerce-2026).
