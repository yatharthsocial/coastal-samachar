Tulu is the heart of the region known as Tulu Nadu, spoken across Dakshina Kannada, Udupi and parts of Kasaragod. It carries centuries of songs, stories and traditions.

## A language rich in oral tradition

Much of Tulu culture has been passed down through spoken word: folk epics called *paddanas*, work songs from the paddy fields, and the stories told during rituals and festivals.

## New life on stage and screen

Tulu theatre and cinema have a loyal following, and young creators are now making short films, comedy videos and music in Tulu for social media audiences.

## Why it matters for students

- Knowing your mother tongue strengthens family and community ties.
- Research shows that multilingual students often find it easier to learn additional languages.
- Local language skills are valued in journalism, teaching, tourism and public service.

## How you can help

1. Speak Tulu at home, especially with children.
2. Watch, share and support Tulu films, plays and music.
3. Join college cultural clubs that celebrate local language and arts.
4. Record the stories and songs of elders before they are lost.

> A language lives as long as people use it every day.
