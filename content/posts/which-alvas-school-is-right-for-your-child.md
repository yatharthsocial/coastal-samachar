Alva's Education Foundation runs a full ladder of schools on its campus in **Moodbidri**, from kindergarten to Class 10, in more than one medium and board. Here is how they fit together.

## The Alva's schools at a glance

According to [Alva's Schools](https://alvasschools.com/), the campus has:

- **Kindergarten**
- **English medium primary and higher primary school**
- **[Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school)** (Standards 8–10, SSLC)
- **Kannada medium higher primary and high school**
- **Alva's ICSE School**
- **Alva's Central School** (NCERT)

## Choosing between them

### For the Karnataka SSLC: English Medium High School

[Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) is the choice for families who want English-medium teaching with the state SSLC exam at the end of Class 10. Its toppers have scored up to **624/625** in the SSLC; it keeps classes to **35 students** and adds **IIT foundation coaching**.

### For Kannada-medium learning

The Kannada medium higher primary and high schools offer the same campus facilities with Kannada as the medium of instruction.

### For other boards

Alva's Central School (NCERT) and Alva's ICSE School are options for families who prefer those curricula.

## What every Alva's student shares

- **Hostels** for boys and girls
- **Sports**, including a 400-metre synthetic track
- **Cultural programmes**, and a role in the foundation's festivals, [Alva's Nudisiri-Virasat](/news/alvas-nudisiri-virasat-2027)
- **A path to Alva's PU College**, whose student [Disha topped Karnataka in Commerce in II PUC 2026](/news/alvas-pu-college-disha-tops-karnataka-commerce-2026)
