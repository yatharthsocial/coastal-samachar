[Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) and its sister Kannada Medium High School in **Moodbidri** have produced some of the highest SSLC scores in Dakshina Kannada. Here are the top scores reported over the years.

## Year by year

### 2018: second in Karnataka

**Pranshula Prashanth** scored **624** to secure the **second rank in the state**. Across the Kannada and English medium schools, **903 students** wrote the exam, **885 passed (98.66%)**, **46 scored above 600**, and **62 scored above 95%**.

### 2017: two students at 623

**Deeksha M N** and **Akash M Nairi** scored **623**. The English Medium High School recorded **99.15%** with **472 students**, and **28 scored above 600**. The Kannada Medium High School recorded **100% results for the eighth year in a row**, with **35 students above 600**.

### 2012–16: the first five batches

The school reported **100% results** in each of its first five batches, with toppers scoring up to **618 (98.88%)** — Gokul Reddy K C in 2014–15 and Kavya Aradhya in 2015–16.

### 2026

**Shadhvi P V** of Alva's School, Moodbidri, scored **622**, as Dakshina Kannada topped the state with a **98.40%** pass rate. [Read more](/news/alvas-school-sslc-2026-result).

## What drives the results

Classes of up to **35 students**, **IIT foundation coaching**, a **study hall** and extra coaching for students who need it. Learn more on our [Alva's English Medium High School page](/alvas-english-medium-high-school).
