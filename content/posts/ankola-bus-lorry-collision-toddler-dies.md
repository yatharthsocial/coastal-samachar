A toddler died and more than 10 passengers were injured when a state-run bus and a lorry collided head-on on **National Highway 66 near Belse village in Ankola taluk** on Thursday, 1 October.

## What happened

The bus was travelling from Kumta towards Ankola when it collided with a lorry coming from the opposite direction, [Sahil Online reported](https://sahilonline.org/boy-dies-over-10-injured-in-bus-lorry-crash-in-ankola-near-bhatkal). The front portions of both vehicles were badly damaged.

## Victims

The child who died has been identified as **Archith Tandel**. The drivers of both vehicles were among the seriously injured. The injured were given first aid at a nearby hospital and later shifted to **KRIMS Hospital, Karwar**, for further treatment.
