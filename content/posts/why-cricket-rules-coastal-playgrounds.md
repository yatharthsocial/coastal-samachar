On any Sunday morning along the coast, you will find a cricket match: on a school ground, a paddy field after harvest or a stretch of firm sand.

## Tennis-ball cricket

Local tournaments played with taped tennis balls draw huge crowds. Matches are short, fast and fiercely competitive.

## A pathway for young talent

Many players start in these informal leagues before joining clubs and academies. Good coaching and grounds remain the biggest needs.

## More than a game

Tournaments raise money for local causes, bring villages together and give young people a reason to stay active.

## Other sports on the rise

Kabaddi, volleyball, football and surfing are growing fast too, especially in schools and colleges.
