**Alva's CBSE school** in **Moodbidri** has recorded **100% results** in the **CBSE Class 10** examinations for the **sixth year in a row**, [Pingara reported](https://pingara.com/kannada-news/alvas-cbse-result-2026/).

**46 students** scored above **95%**, **116** above **90%** and **225** above **80%**. Alva's Education Foundation chairman **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** said Alva's ranks first in Karnataka for the number of students scoring above 95% from a single school, according to the report.

## Perfect scores in subjects

- **IT:** 17 students scored 100
- **Sanskrit:** 7 students
- **Hindi:** 4 students
- **Kannada:** 3 students
- **Science** and **English:** 1 student each

## One campus, two exam paths

Alva's Education Foundation runs both a CBSE school — listed by the foundation as **Alva's Central School (NCERT)** — and [**Alva's English Medium High School**](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) for the Karnataka SSLC, on the same Moodbidri campus.
