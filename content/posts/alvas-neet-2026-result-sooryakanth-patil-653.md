Students of **Alva's PU College, Moodbidri**, posted strong results in **NEET 2026**. **Sooryakanth Patil** scored **653 marks**, and **21 students** scored above the **99th percentile**, [Pingara reported](https://pingara.com/kannada-news/neet-2026-exam-results-unprecedented-achievement-by-alvas-students/).

## By the numbers

- **10 students** above 600 marks
- **161 students** above 500 marks
- **467 students** above 450 marks
- **21 students** above the 99th percentile

In category ranks, Sooryakanth Patil was placed **97th** nationally, Chethan Yaranal **239th**, Clarence D'Souza **724th** and Deekshitha K.S **951st**, according to the college. Alva's expects **more than 450 students** to secure **government-quota MBBS seats**.

Students who want to stay close to home can now study medicine at Moodbidri too: the new [Alva's Institute of Medical Sciences and Research Centre](/news/alvas-medical-college-opens-in-moodbidri) has **150 MBBS seats** from 2026–27.
