**Ten students** of **Alva's Pre-University College, Moodbidri**, have cleared the **National Defence Academy (NDA) and Naval Academy (NA)** entrance examination conducted by the **UPSC**, according to [Alva's PU College](https://alvaspucollege.org/blog/proud-achievement-10-students-of-alvas-qualify-in-nda-examination/). The achievement was highlighted in *Samyukta Karnataka* on 13 October 2026.

## What comes next

The written exam is the first stage. The students must now clear the **Services Selection Board (SSB)** interview and **medical examinations** before final selection.

## Training for defence careers

Alva's provides **special training** for students preparing for the NDA. Chairman **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** congratulated the students.

Alva's students also take part in **NCC** and **Scouts and Guides** from school onwards — see our [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) page.
