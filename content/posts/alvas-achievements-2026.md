2026 has been a standout year for students of **Alva's Education Foundation** in **Moodbidri**. Here is everything they achieved — in one place.

## Sports

- **State U-17 Athletics 2026 — overall champions.** Alva's School won **26 medals (11 gold, 8 silver, 7 bronze)** and both team titles at Kanteerava Stadium, Bengaluru. **12 athletes** qualified for the nationals. [Full results](/news/alvas-school-state-u17-athletics-champions-2026)
- **South Zone Junior Athletics — 26 medals, 5 meet records.** [Read more](/news/alvas-athletes-26-medals-south-zone-junior-athletics)

## SSLC

- **622 in SSLC 2026:** Shadhvi P V of Alva's School, Moodbidri. [Read more](/news/alvas-school-sslc-2026-result)
- **Best ever: 624/625**, Pranshula Prashanth (2018), second in Karnataka. [All Alva's SSLC toppers](/news/alvas-sslc-toppers-highest-scores)

## II PUC and entrance exams

- **State first in Commerce:** Disha, **600/600**, II PUC 2026. [Read more](/news/alvas-pu-college-disha-tops-karnataka-commerce-2026)
- **JEE Advanced 2026:** **17 qualified**, best rank AIR 1513 (Swathi Talwar). [Read more](/news/alvas-pu-jee-advanced-2026-17-qualify)
- **NDA and NA:** **10 students** cleared the UPSC written exam. [Read more](/news/alvas-pu-students-clear-nda-exam-2026)

## Culture

- **Alva's Nudisiri-Virasat 2027** — the 17th Nudisiri and 31st Virasat — will be held in Moodbidri from **6 to 10 January 2027**, with student festivals *Vidyarthisiri* and *Yuvasiri* and a state-level Scouts and Guides Jamboree. [Read more](/news/alvas-nudisiri-virasat-2027)
- Alva's students learn **classical and folk arts, dance and music**, and the foundation's festivals include dedicated student days such as *Vidyarthisiri*.
