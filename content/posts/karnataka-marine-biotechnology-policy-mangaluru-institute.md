The Karnataka government has approved the **Karnataka Marine Biotechnology Policy** to harness the state's marine resources and promote the **blue economy**, Udupi district in-charge Minister **Rizwan Arshad** said at the Kannada Rajyotsava celebrations in Udupi on 1 November, [Mangalore Today reported](https://www.mangaloretoday.com/main/Udupi-Karnataka-Marine-Biotechnology-Policy-Approved-to-Harness-Coastal-Wealth-Rizwan-Arshad.html).

## New institute in Mangaluru

Under the policy, a **Karnataka Institute of Marine Technology and Blue Economy** will be set up at the **Government Fisheries College, Mangaluru**. The college's intake will rise from **60 to 200 seats**.

## What it means for students

The policy is expected to open new opportunities in **marine research, education and economic development** — good news for students on the coast interested in fisheries, marine science and biotechnology after PUC.

## Tourism plans for Udupi

The minister also announced a resort, amusement parks and sports facilities on **7.07 acres at Trasi and Maravanthe beaches**, at an estimated **₹48 crore**.
