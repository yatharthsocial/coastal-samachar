The Tulu Nadu region has one of the richest cultural calendars in India. Here's a season-by-season guide. Exact dates change every year, so always check local announcements.

## Monsoon and early autumn

### Krishna Janmashtami in Udupi

Udupi celebrates Krishna Janmashtami with great energy, including the colourful *Vittal Pindi* procession and street performances.

### Ganesha Chaturthi

Neighbourhoods and organisations install Ganesha idols, with music, cultural programmes and community meals.

## Navaratri and Dasara

### Mangaluru Dasara

Mangaluru Dasara is famous for its grand procession and illuminated streets, drawing visitors from across the state.

### Pili Vesha

During Navaratri, troupes of *Pili Vesha* (tiger dance) performers, painted in bold stripes, dance through the streets to drum beats. It is one of the most photographed sights on the coast.

## Winter to spring

### Kambala season

Traditional buffalo races in slushy paddy fields take place in many villages during the dry months, roughly from November to March.

### Yakshagana season

Touring Yakshagana troupes perform through the night in temple grounds and open fields. See our [beginner's guide to Yakshagana](/news/yakshagana-guide-for-first-time-viewers).

### Temple jatres and car festivals

Village and temple fairs (*jatre*) and chariot festivals (*rathotsava*) bring markets, rides and food stalls.

## Tips for visitors

1. Follow local customs and dress modestly at temple events.
2. Ask before photographing performers and devotees.
3. Plan for crowds and parking — arrive early.
