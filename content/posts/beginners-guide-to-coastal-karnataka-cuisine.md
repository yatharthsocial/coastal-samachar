Coastal Karnataka's food is built on coconut, rice, fresh seafood and a confident hand with spices. Here is where to start.

## Neer dosa

Thin, soft and lacy, neer dosa is made from a watery rice batter. It pairs with everything from coconut chutney to chicken curry.

## Kori rotti

A Mangalorean classic: crisp, dry rice wafers soaked in a rich chicken curry just before eating.

## Fish curry and rice

Every home has its own version, usually built on coconut, tamarind and red chillies. Mackerel, sardines and kingfish are popular choices.

## Goli baje

Soft, fluffy fritters made with maida and curd — the perfect evening snack with a cup of filter coffee.

## Udupi vegetarian fare

Udupi's temple cuisine gave the world the masala dosa. Look out for *saaru* (a thin rasam), *gojju* and banana-leaf meals.

## Tips for first-timers

- Ask for the spice level — coastal curries can be fiery.
- Try the fish of the day at a small local restaurant.
- Finish with *kadubu* or a glass of fresh sugarcane juice.
