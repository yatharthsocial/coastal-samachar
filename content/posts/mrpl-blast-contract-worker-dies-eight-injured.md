A contract worker died and eight others were injured after an explosion and fire at the **Mangalore Refinery and Petrochemicals Limited (MRPL)** facility at Jokatte, Mangaluru, on Wednesday, 30 September.

## What happened

The blast took place around 12:15 pm in the refinery's **Coker Hydrotreater Unit (Phase-3)**, where a cold separator operating at high pressure ruptured, [ThePrint reported](https://theprint.in/india/one-killed-eight-injured-in-mrpl-oil-refinery-explosion-fir-registered/3058471/). The explosion was heard several kilometres away, and some nearby houses reported damaged windows and doors.

## Worker killed

The deceased has been identified as **Manish Karkera** of Mulki, a contract worker. MRPL said his body was found at the site during inspection after the fire was put out. One of the injured is in critical condition with burn injuries.

## Case registered

Surathkal police have registered an FIR against the head of the Coker Hydrotreater Unit and MRPL management under sections of the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita relating to negligence.
