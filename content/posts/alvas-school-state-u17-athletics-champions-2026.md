Representing **Dakshina Kannada**, **Alva's School, Moodubidire**, was crowned **overall champion** at the **State-level Under-17 Boys and Girls Athletics Championship 2026**, winning **26 medals** — **11 gold, 8 silver and 7 bronze** — and the **team titles in both the boys' and girls' categories**, [Pingara reported](https://pingara.com/kannada-news/26-medals-for-alvas-school-12-athletes-qualify-for-national-level/).

The meet was held at **Sri Kanteerava Stadium, Bengaluru**, from **1 to 3 November**. **Twelve Alva's athletes** have qualified for the **National School Athletics Championship** in Madhya Pradesh.

Alva's athletes also won gold in the boys' pole vault and silver in the boys' triple jump.

## Wishes for the nationals

Alva's Education Foundation chairman **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** congratulated the team and wished the 12 national qualifiers success.
