Yakshagana blends dance, music, dialogue and costume into a performance that can last the whole night. Here is what to expect.

## The stories

Most performances are drawn from the Ramayana, the Mahabharata and the Puranas. Each story, called a *prasanga*, is well known to regular audiences.

## The music

The *bhagavata* (lead singer) narrates the story, backed by the *chende* and *maddale* drums. The singer sets the pace for the whole show.

## The costumes

Huge headgear, bright face paint and layered costumes help the audience identify heroes, demons and gods at a glance.

## Tips for first-timers

- Read the story beforehand so you can follow along.
- Arrive early for a good seat.
- Don't worry about understanding every word — the drama speaks for itself.
