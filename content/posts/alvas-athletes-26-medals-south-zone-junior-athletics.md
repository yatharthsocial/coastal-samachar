Athletes from **Alva's Education Foundation**, Moodbidri, won **26 medals** and set **five new meet records** at the **South Zone Junior Athletics Championship**, [Udayavani reported](https://udayavani.com/karnataka/alvas-athletes-win-26-medals-set-five-new-meet-records-466181?lang=en).

## Built on a strong sports programme

Sport is a big part of life at Alva's. The campus has a **400-metre synthetic athletics track** and separate coaches for different events. At [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school), a **sports quota** for Standards 8 to 10 offers free food, accommodation and coaching to selected athletes.

## How to join the sports quota

Notifications are published in newspapers in **March–April**. Selected students attend a 20-day camp at the foundation before final selection. See our [admissions guide](/news/alvas-english-medium-high-school-admissions-guide) for the full steps.
