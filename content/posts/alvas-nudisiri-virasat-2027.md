**Alva's Nudisiri** and **Alva's Virasat** will be held together as a five-day cultural and literary festival — **Alva's Nudisiri-Virasat** — in Moodbidri from **6 to 10 January 2027**, [NewsKarnataka reported](https://newskarnataka.com/mangaluru/alvas-nudisiri-virasat-to-be-held-in-moodbidri-from-january-6-to-10/09102026/).

Alva's Education Foundation chairman **Dr. M. Mohan Alva** announced the dates at a press conference in October. This will be the **17th Nudisiri** and the **31st Virasat**.

## Theme

The central theme is **"Kannada Manassu: Bhavishyada Kanasu"**.

## Highlights

- **6 January:** Virasat opens with a cultural chariot procession and a Ganga Aarti by a troupe from Haridwar. Two Virasat Awards will be presented.
- **8 January:** *Alva's Vidyarthisiri*, for students.
- **9 January:** *Alva's Yuvasiri*, for young people.
- **Nudisiri literary convention:** presided over by literary critic **Narahalli Balasubrahmanya** and inaugurated by senior journalist **Vishweshwar Bhat**.
- **State-level Scouts and Guides Jamboree**, with more than 5,000 participants expected.
- **Five exhibitions** on agriculture, science, food, literature and art.
- **Free fine arts training** for about 800 artists.

## Students at the centre

Students from [Alva's English Medium High School](/alvas-english-medium-high-school) and the foundation's other institutions take part in cultural programmes, Scouts and Guides and the student festivals every year.
